## Reporting a Vulnerability

Use this section to tell people how to report a vulnerability.

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in Material Home Assistant, we encourage you to let us know right away. We will investigate all legitimate reports and do our best to quickly fix the problem.

### Reporting

Please report security vulnerabilities to [giovannilamarmora.working@gmail.com](mailto:giovannilamarmora.working@gmail.com).

### Response

We will respond to your report within 5 business days.

### Disclosure

We will disclose the vulnerability to the public once a fix has been released.

### Bounty

We do not offer a bug bounty program at this time.

### Acknowledgements

We will acknowledge your contribution in the release notes of the fix.
